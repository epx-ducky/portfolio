# Comonut Context

Comonut is a digital platform for municipalities to revive local community activities, connect citizens, coordinate youth-led activities, and integrate local businesses.

## Language

### Core Entities

**Municipality (Gemeinde)**:
The local government entity (city, village, town) that subscribes to the Comonut platform and manages local resources.
*Avoid*: Stadt, Dorf, Kommune (in code/database schemas, use Municipality)

**Resident (Einwohner)**:
A citizen living in the municipality who uses the app to find and participate in activities.
*Avoid*: Bürger, User, Client

**Orga-Team Member (Orga-Team-Mitglied)**:
A young adult (aged 16-22) contracted by the municipality to organize activities for children and coordinate facility access.
*Avoid*: Betreuer, Animator, Worker

**Local Business (Lokaler Betrieb)**:
A local shop, craftsman, restaurant, or service provider operating within the municipality that can promote activities or offers.
*Avoid*: Geschäft, Laden, Partner

### Activities & Booking

**Activity (Aktivität)**:
A scheduled event, game, or meetup (e.g., volleyball, cycling tour, crafts) that residents can join.
*Avoid*: Event, Spiel, Treffen

**Facility (Sportstätte/Halle)**:
A physical location owned or managed by the municipality (soccer field, gym, playground, community center) that can be booked for activities.
*Avoid*: Location, Ort, Platz

**Slot (Zeitfenster)**:
A specific block of time reserved for an activity at a facility.
*Avoid*: Termin, Buchung

**Bulletin Board (Schwarzes Brett)**:
A digital board for announcements, local news, and community notifications posted by the municipality or local clubs.
*Avoid*: Newsfeed, Pinnwand, Forum

### Community & Information

**Local Gazette (Dorfblatt)**:
The digital version of the municipality's official printed newsletter or newspaper, uploaded as a PDF or interactive articles.
*Avoid*: Amtsblatt, Zeitung, Gemeindeblatt

**Community Feed (Dorf-Newsfeed)**:
A chronological stream of official municipality updates, emergency alerts, and community posts.
*Avoid*: Timeline, Status

**Damage Report (Mängelmelder)**:
A citizen-reported issue (e.g., broken street lamp, pothole) sent directly to the municipality administration.
*Avoid*: Schadensbericht, Ticket

### Commerce & Gamification

**Deal (Gewerbe-Aktion)**:
A special offer, discount, or promotion created by a local business for residents (e.g., lunch specials, flash discounts).
*Avoid*: Coupon, Rabatt, Angebot

**Community Project (Gemeinschaftsprojekt)**:
A crowd-funded or volunteer-driven local project (e.g., renovating a playground) sponsored by local businesses or the municipality.
*Avoid*: Kampagne, Initiative
